# UX review, 2026-10-11

Tested on `main` plus the `chore/ux-reviewer-agent` branch, local dev server, seeded dev database (10+ products, postal code 0150). Viewport 375x812 (phone) in dark and light mode, one pass at 1280 px wide.

Roles tried: logged out visitor, `USER`, `PRODUCT_ADMIN`. `ADMIN` has the same guard as `PRODUCT_ADMIN` today, so it was not tried separately.

**Not tested:** the camera scan (the browser pane has no camera), Google login, a real phone, the production build, slow networks.

Each finding has a priority: **P1** blocks the app's purpose or damages data, **P2** clear friction, **P3** polish. "Where" points to the page, "Idea" is a suggestion, not a decision.

## Summary

The core loop works and feels fast: search, product page, add to list, plan cards. The shopping list plans are the best part of the app. The biggest risks are not visual. They are about **trust in the data** (anyone can overwrite a price with nonsense and nobody can undo it) and **the empty start** (a new user in an unseeded area, or with a product not in the catalogue, hits a dead end).

Top five to do first:
1. Sanity check on reported prices, and let the reporter fix their own report (U1).
2. Unknown barcode must not be a dead end (U2).
3. Show prices from nearby postal codes instead of nothing (U3).
4. Admins need a way to remove a bad price report (A1).
5. Show how much the user saves (U4).

## Regular user (`USER`)

Needs: find out fast where this product is cheapest, plan one shopping trip, and add prices without effort while standing in the store.

### P1

**U1. Any price is accepted, and the latest report wins at once.**
Where: product page, "Rapporter pris".
Seen: I reported 2990 kr for 1 l of milk at Kiwi. It was saved and became the Kiwi price immediately ("Rapportert i dag", 2 990,00 kr). That price also fed into the shopping list totals. The reporter cannot edit or undo it, and no one else can see that it is wrong. (I restored Kiwi to 22,90 in the dev data afterwards.)
Why it matters: DECISIONS #2 says latest wins. One typo (`2990` instead of `29,90`) or one bad actor breaks the number the whole app is built on.
Idea: (a) reject or ask "Er du sikker?" when the price is far from the existing prices for that product (for example more than 3x or less than 1/3 of the median), (b) show "Du rapporterte denne, angre" for a few minutes after submitting, (c) later: the consensus idea already in `docs/domain/pricing.md`.

**U2. Scanning an unknown barcode is a dead end.**
Where: `/scan`, after a lookup with no match.
Seen: "Fant ikke produktet med strekkode 7038010000000. Vi legger det til etter hvert." Two buttons: "Skann ny" and "Søk". Nothing else.
Why it matters: scanning is the first thing the landing page promises ("Skann"). With a small curated catalogue, many real scans will miss. The user did the work, then gets nothing, and the app learns nothing.
Idea: until proposals are built (roadmap "Later"), at least record the missed barcode (a count per barcode gives admins a to-do list, see A2) and tell the user "Takk, vi har notert den". The "Søk" button should say what it does (search by name?).

**U3. A postal code without data shows nothing, even next to a postal code with data.**
Where: product page, shopping list.
Seen: with postal code 7000 the product page says "Ingen har rapportert pris for postnummer 7000 ennå." and the shopping list says "Ingen pris i 7000 ennå" on every item. A neighbour of 0150 would show the same.
Why it matters: at launch almost every postal code is empty. A new user will see an empty app and leave. This is the cold start problem, and DECISIONS #3 already names the fix (nearest area with data, labelled), it is just not built.
Idea: this is the most valuable "Later" item. A cheap first version: use the first 2 or 3 digits of the postal code as the fallback area, labelled "Priser fra 01xx".

### P2

**U4. The app never says how much you save.**
Where: shopping list summary and plan cards.
Seen: "Billigst: Rema 1000, 71,80 kr". Plan 2 is 72,80 kr.
Why: the promise on the landing page is "finn butikken som lønner seg". A number like "Du sparer 4 kr mot dyreste kjede" or "1 kr billigere enn Kiwi" is the reward for using the app, and the reason to report prices.
Idea: show the difference to the most expensive complete plan on the summary bar. Show a per-product "billigst hos" tag on the list items.

**U5. Plans 2 and 3 can be worse in every way.**
Where: "Billigste butikk".
Seen: "Kiwi og Rema 1000 · 2 stopp, 72,80 kr" and "Meny og Rema 1000 · 2 stopp, 75,80 kr", both more expensive than the 1-stop Rema plan, with more stops.
Why: more cards, no real choice. Up to 9 cards for 9 chains (already noted as "accepted" in the shopping list doc).
Idea: hide a plan that is both more expensive and has the same or more stops than a plan above it. Or show the first 3 and a "Vis alle".

**U6. Search results carry no price and no explanation for loose matches.**
Where: `/search`.
Seen: searching "melk" lists Lettmelk, then "Mel hvete 2 kg" and "Smør 250 g". The loose matches look like errors, and no result shows a price.
Why: the user searches to compare. They have to open every result.
Idea: show the cheapest price and chain on each row ("fra 21,90 kr, Rema 1000"). Group the fuzzy matches under a small "Lignende treff" heading.

**U7. The empty search page is blank.**
Where: `/search` with no query.
Seen: a field and a very large empty area.
Idea: short hint ("Søk på navn eller merke") and, once built, recent searches, "Dine siste varer" or the shopping list.

**U8. Stale success message on the report form.**
Where: product page.
Seen: after a successful report, "Takk! Prisen er rapportert." stays. When I then submitted without choosing a chain, the old thank you was still shown while the form was reset to empty. The chain select is reset, the price field keeps its old text in some cases.
Idea: clear the message as soon as the user edits a field, and reset both fields together.

**U9. No confirmation when the postal code is saved.**
Where: `/profile`.
Seen: pressing "Lagre" re-enables the button and nothing else changes. I only knew it worked by checking another page.
Idea: "Lagret" text or a toast, and show the place name next to the code ("0150 Oslo"). The place name also protects against typos in the postal code.

**U10. The product page has no "back", no cheapest marker and no price context.**
Where: `/products/[id]`.
Seen: the list of chains is sorted cheapest first, but nothing marks the winner. Only 3 of 9 chains show, with no hint that the others are unknown. The postal code in "Priser i 0150" is not a link to change it.
Idea: "Billigst" tag on the first row, a line "Ingen pris ennå for Spar, Coop ..." that doubles as a call to report, and "Endre" next to the postal code.

**U11. Reporting is slower than it needs to be in a store.**
Where: report form.
Seen: dropdown for chain, free text price, submit. For one scan the user must report 1 product and 1 chain. After the scan, the app does not remember the last chain used.
Idea: remember the last chain, and offer the chain buttons (most used first) instead of a select. After a scan of a known product, jump straight to the price field.

### P3

- **U12. Ticks look like "bought".** The checkboxes on the list only dim an item, but visually look like "bought". The list doc says this is intended. A user in a store will expect a strikethrough and the item sinking to the bottom.
- **U13. No count badge on the "Handleliste" tab** (already in "Not built"). A small number would show the list is alive.
- **U14. Landing page has no screenshots or example of a price comparison**, only icons and text. A real product card ("Lettmelk 1 l, fra 21,90 kr") would sell the idea faster.
- **U15. Layout is not constrained on desktop.** At 1280 px wide, `main` and the bottom nav are 1280 px wide, the lists stretch the full width and the nav items are far apart. Fine if the app is phone only, but then say so with a max width (for example 480 px, centred), since link previews and desktop visitors will see it.
- **U16. Unbranded 404.** `/products/unknown` and `/admin` as a regular user show the default English "404 This page could not be found." No link back. A Norwegian "Fant ikke siden" with a link to the search page is enough.
- **U17. Accessibility.** The price field's accessible name is its placeholder ("29,90"), so the "Pris i kroner" label is not linked to the input. The page has two things called "Rapporter pris" (heading and button). On the report form the submit button sits right above the bottom nav and is partly hidden after an error message appears, so the user has to scroll.
- **U18. Inconsistent side padding.** Search, product, list use 16 px, profile and admin pages use 24 px.

## Product admin (`PRODUCT_ADMIN`, and `ADMIN`)

Needs: grow and clean the catalogue quickly, ideally while holding the product, and keep prices trustworthy.

### P1

**A1. No way to remove or correct a price report.**
Where: nowhere.
Seen: the admin has `/admin/products` (list, create, edit) and nothing for reports. The 2 990 kr report from U1 can only be fixed by reporting a new price, or in Prisma Studio.
Why: with "latest wins", moderation is the only quality control there is. Without it, the admin can only watch.
Idea: on the product page, for admins only, a small "Skjul" or "Slett" per price row (soft delete flag so history stays), and a list of the newest reports across all products (`/admin/reports`) to scan for oddities.

### P2

**A2. No link between the scanner and the catalogue.**
Where: `/scan`, unknown barcode.
Seen: an admin who scans an unknown barcode gets the same dead end as a user. They must remember the number, go to Profil, Administrer produkter, Nytt produkt, and type it again.
Idea: for admin roles, the not-found message gets a "Opprett produkt" button that opens the form with the barcode filled in. Also the new form could have a "Skann strekkode" button that fills the barcode field with the camera, since the admin is likely standing with the product.

**A3. The admin list cannot be searched or filtered.**
Where: `/admin/products`.
Seen: one alphabetical list of all products, no count, no search, no paging. Fine for 19 products, not for 2 000.
Idea: reuse the search field from `/search`. Add filters that matter to a curator: "uten priser", "uten merke", "uten beskrivelse". Show the total.

**A4. Admin tools are hidden.**
Where: profile page.
Seen: the only entry is the "Administrer produkter" button on the profile page. The role is not shown anywhere ("Dev PRODUCT_ADMIN" only appears because of the dev account name). An admin looking at a product page in the app has no "Rediger" shortcut.
Idea: on `/products/[id]`, an "Rediger produkt" link for admins. Show the role on the profile ("Produktadmin").

**A5. The product form does not explain its one hard rule.**
Where: `/admin/products/new` and edit.
Seen: "Strekkode" and "PLU-kode" both look optional or required, with no hint. Saving with only a name gives "Produktet trenger strekkode eller PLU-kode" at the bottom of the form, below the fold on a phone.
Idea: put "Strekkode eller PLU-kode (én av dem må fylles ut)" above the two fields, and show the error next to them. The duplicate error ("Et produkt med denne strekkoden finnes allerede") should link to the existing product.

### P3

- **A6. No delete or archive** (known, "Not built"). Even a soft "Skjul fra søk" would help fix mistakes made while testing.
- **A7. No image**, and no way to see what the user sees. A preview of how the product looks in search and on the product page would help.
- **A8. "Lagre" then back to the list.** For bulk entry, "Lagre og legg til neste" saves a lot of taps. Keep the weight and brand of the last one as defaults only if it proves useful.
- **A9. Weight and volume are optional and separate.** For per-kg or per-litre comparison later, the form could also ask for a unit (g, ml, stk). Not needed yet, but cheaper to ask now than to fix 2 000 products later.
- **A10. The description field is visually different** from the others (larger, brighter placeholder). It has the right purpose (search), but a short line "Brukes bare i søk, vises ikke for brukere" would stop admins from writing marketing text there.

## Environment notes found while testing

Not UX, but they cost time and are worth fixing:

- `.env.development.local` contains `ENABLE_DEV_TOOLS=trueF` (stray "F"). The dev login returns 404 because of it. I did not change the file. I started the server with `ENABLE_DEV_TOOLS=true` set on the command line instead.
- A dev server started by the browser pane (`preview_start`) could not reach the Neon dev database ("Can't reach database server"), while the same URLs worked from the shell and from a dev server started in the shell. Cause not found. Starting `npm run dev` from the shell and opening it in the pane works.
- `.claude/launch.json` did not exist and I created an untracked one, which is not useful given the item above. Safe to delete.

## Suggested order

1. U1, A1 (trust in prices). Small: range check plus an admin hide button.
2. U2, A2 (unknown barcode). Record the miss, and give admins a shortcut.
3. U3 (nearby prices), then U4 and U6 (show the saving and the price in search).
4. P2 polish in one pass: U8, U9, U10, U11, A3, A4, A5.
5. P3 items when touching those screens anyway.

### Laptop layout (P3, U15)

The owner finds the desktop view bad: very long buttons, stretched rows. Mobile is fine and should not change.

Proposed fix, to be checked on a real laptop screen:
- Centre the page content in a column with a max width (about 640 px for lists and forms, 768 px for the shopping list).
- Buttons keep their natural width on wide screens (only full width below the `sm` breakpoint).
- The bottom nav also gets the max width, centred, or moves to a top bar on wide screens. Decide after trying both.
- Admin pages use the same column, then a wider table layout for the product overview (below).

### Product admin

- **Admin dashboard (A3).** Agreed. One page with all current products and their prices. Suggested content:
  - A table of products with the number of chains that have a price, the cheapest price, and the age of the newest report.
  - Filters: "uten priser", "gamle priser (over 30 dager)", "uten merke".
  - Search by name or barcode.
  - The scanned-misses list from U2 as a second tab, with a "Opprett produkt" button per barcode.
  - On wide screens this is a table. On a phone it stays a list.
- **Scan to add a product (A2).** Agreed. Two entry points:
  - "Skann strekkode" button on the new-product form, which fills the barcode field.
  - On `/scan`, an unknown barcode shows "Opprett produkt" for admin roles, with the barcode already filled in.
- **Price moderation (A1)** was not discussed. It still follows from U1: until there is a median, an admin needs a way to hide a bad report. Suggest putting it on the dashboard.

### Suggested build order

1. **Price trust:** U1 confirm dialog, U8, U9 (small, all on existing forms).
2. **Laptop layout** (P3, U15). One change in the shared layout, so it improves every page.
3. **List and search:** U4 saving, U5 first 3 plans, U6 price on search results, U7 hint.
4. **Scan misses and admin scan:** U2 table, A2 scan to add, "Opprett produkt" from a miss.
5. **Admin dashboard** (A3), with price hiding (A1).
6. **U11 favourites and last used chains** (needs a schema change on the user).
7. **Later:** U3 nearby prices, median or same-price count, search statistics, price history.

Each step should become its own roadmap item and PR, per the project rules. Steps 2 and 5 are the largest and may need a designer handover file in `docs/handover/` first.
